### 🐉 Why _Elden Ring_ Feels Like an Ankle-Rolling Chore (And Why Yours Won't)

In _Elden Ring_, fighting a giant often degenerates into staring at kneecaps and spamming slow, stamina-draining rolls because your character lacks vertical mobility and reactive deflection.

  

_Sekiro_ (with **Guardian Ape** and **Demon of Hatred**) and _Phantom Blade Zero_ avoid that trap completely by giving you **explosive dash traction, crisp jump-evades, and selective deflection**—turning a colossal monster into an exhilarating spatial dance rather than a slow roll-fest.

### ⚔️ 3 AAA Rules to Make Colossal Titans Fit Fast Deflection Combat

Drawing directly from _Sekiro_, _Monster Hunter_, and _Lies of P_, you can make a towering _Tower Knight_ or _Giant Lord_ feel completely cohesive inside your high-octane combat system:

  

1. **Allow Deflections on Weapon Edges, Ban Them on Body Slams (_Sekiro_'s Guardian Ape Rule):**
    
    - Nobody expects you to parry a giant dropping its entire stone torso onto your head—that stays a strict **`Tier 4: COLOSSAL`** slam that must be evaded via _Ghoststep_ or sprint.
        
    - However, letting the player **deflect the sweeping edge of a giant's glaive or forearm** with a massive shower of `AgX` sparks feels heroic, rewards bravery, and chunks their **Poise** bar dramatically.
        
2. **Turn Ground Shockwaves Into Jump & _Ghoststep_ Counters (_Phantom Blade Zero_ Flow):**
    
    - Instead of rolling blindly into a giant's shins, design colossal sweeps and radial earth-slams to be **jumped over or _Ghoststepped_ diagonally**—letting you stay aggressive in mid-air with downward plunge slashes while keeping the titan's upper silhouette framed cleanly on camera.
        
3. **Break Ankles to Bring the Head Down (_Monster Hunter_ Topple Loop):**
    
    - Just as _Monster Hunter_ hunters focus heavy `CRUSH` blows on a wyvern's legs to trigger a knockdown, give colossal bosses **high `base_armour_tier`** so they never flinch from random pokes—until your sustained combos and `F` Boss Arts fill their **Poise** bar and crash their head straight onto the arena floor for a lethal `execution_multiplier` riposte.
        

### 🎯 The Solo-Dev Scope Guardrail

Keep **80% of your enemy roster** in the fast-to-medium humanoid and armoured-knight lane (_Onze_, _Seeker_, _Crucible / Black Knight_) where your 3-hit katana loop and deflection engine shine brightest.

  

Treat **1 or 2 colossal titans** (_Tower Knight_ or _High Lord Wolnir_) as rare, unforgettable regional set-pieces—giving players a thrilling change of pace without forcing you to rig a dozen sprawling giants.
