Great open-world and semi-open action games reward exploration on **two distinct levels**: **extrinsic progression** (tangibly making your character stronger or unlocking shortcuts) and **intrinsic curiosity** (solving spatial mysteries, discovering environmental stories, and soaking in dramatic vistas).

  

Your idea of tying **higher XP/currency yields to clearing distinct spawn zones per lunar night cycle** is genuinely smart—it turns the entire map into a "hunt circuit" and kills mindless spawn-camp grinding.

  

### 1. How _Elden Ring_, _Breath of the Wild_, and _Sekiro_ Reward Exploration

|**Game**|**Main POI Rewards (Bosses & Major Landmarks)**|**Pure-Curiosity Side Areas ("No Reason" Detours)**|
|---|---|---|
|**_Elden Ring_**|**Build-defining gear & runes:** Legendary weapons, Spirit Ashes, Great Runes, and Smithing Stone Bell Bearings that permanently expand upgrade tiers.|**Environmental storytelling & micro-discoveries:** Hidden cliffside tombs, silent wandering merchants playing music, NPC questlines tucked behind illusory walls, and sweeping underground reveals (like the Siofra River elevator).|
|**_Breath of the Wild_**|**Core survivability & tools:** Spirit Orbs (Heart/Stamina upgrades), Korok Seeds (inventory slots), and Champion abilities.|**Playful spatial puzzles:** Climbing a weirdly shaped peak just to find a Korok under a rock, shielding down a snowy slope, or stumbling onto the Lord of the Mountain glowing in a hidden grove.|
|**_Sekiro_**|**Direct combat power:** Prayer Beads (max Vitality/Posture), Memories (Attack Power), and Prosthetic Tools.|**Tactical reconnaissance & eavesdropping:** Grappling onto rooftops to overhear guards reveal a mini-boss's weakness, finding alternate stealth routes behind patrols, and unlocking **shortcuts** (doors/bridges) back to Sculptor's Idols.|

**The Key Takeaway:** Never make a side path feel like a dead end. Even if a player climbs a steep ridge or explores a burned alley purely out of curiosity, reward them with **at least one** of three things:

  

1. A **one-way shortcut** (kicking down a ladder or unbarring a gate back to the main street).
    
2. A **tactical advantage** (high ground overlooking an enemy patrol or a stealth drop-attack perch).
    
3. A **memorable visual/lore beat** (a ruined shrine overlooking the distant citadel, a dying soldier's note, or a rare consumable).
    

### 2. Why Your "Unique Zone EXP + Nightly Reset" Idea Works (And How to Polish It)

Tying XP/spirit rewards to clearing **unique spawn zones per lunar cycle** directly prevents players from sitting beside a Shrine and killing the same 3 weak enemies 100 times:

  

- **First-Clear Bounty per Lunar Cycle:** Each distinct sub-zone (e.g., _Lower Burned Village_, _Collapsed Aqueduct_, _Ash Courtyard_, _Cliffside Shrine_) awards **100% full XP/Spirit** the first time you clear its patrol during a lunar day/night cycle.
    
- **Diminishing Returns on Repeats (Before Midnight):** If you die or re-trigger the same zone before the Antikythera clock crosses **Midnight (`00:00`)**, repeat kills yield **15–20% XP** (enough for minor pocket change, too low to be worth farming).
    
- **Midnight Reset (`00:00` Pulse):** When the lunar clock crosses midnight, all zone bounties refresh—and **nocturnal elite variants** spawn in 1 or 2 zones with bonus drop rates, encouraging players to roam different corners of the basin each night.
    

### 3. Should You Reward Revisiting Areas Later? (Yes—Without Busywork)

Revisiting earlier regions feels great when the **world or your capabilities change**, rather than forcing tedious backtracking:

1. **Movement / Tool Unlocks (_Sekiro_ / Metroidvania Gateways):** Place a few visible but unreachable ledges, grapple anchors, or sealed iron gates in Region 1 (like an upper burned pagoda balcony or a sunken cellar) that you can only open after obtaining a tool or key in Region 2.
    
2. **World-State Shifts After Major Bosses:** After defeating Region 1's Castle Boss, have a wandering merchant or survivor move into the previously empty _Burned Garrison Outpost_, or shift the region's weather from heavy ash-smoke to cold moonlight—revealing previously obscured side paths.
    
3. **Nocturnal-Only Encounters:** Certain rare mini-bosses or spirit apparitions only appear in familiar landmarks (like the _130 m Sky-Terrace_ or _Cemetery Overlook_) between `00:00` and `04:00` on the Antikythera clock, giving players a concrete reason to revisit scenic spots at night.

_Tears of the Kingdom_ definitely elevates _Breath of the Wild_’s exploration rewards—mostly by shifting from "stumbling into a standalone shrine" to **multi-layered spatial discovery** and **modular gear/tool progression**.

  

### 1. Where _Tears of the Kingdom_ Beats _Breath of the Wild_ at Rewarding Exploration

- **Vertical Sightlines & Multi-Tiered Layers (Sky → Surface → Depths):** Instead of rewarding curiosity only on a horizontal plane, _TotK_ lets you spot a mysterious glowing chasm or floating island from miles away, glide or build a contraption to reach it, and discover an entirely different ecosystem underneath. Even exploring a random well on the surface frequently drops you into a hidden cave network with glowing Bubbulfrogs and armor pieces.
    
- **Functional Crafting & Modular Rewards (Zonai Devices, Schematics & Sage Wills):** Rather than handing out fragile weapons that break after two fights (one of _BotW_’s biggest reward flaws), _TotK_ rewards off-the-beaten-path exploration with **Autobuild Schematics**, **Zonai capsules**, **Battery upgrades**, and **Armor sets** with unique passive bonuses—rewards that permanently expand _how_ you traverse and fight.
    
- **Environmental Breadcrumbs That Lead to Real Payoffs:** Seeing a column of smoke, a cherry blossom tree illuminating nearby cave entrances, or a strange rock formation on a cliffside almost always leads to a handcrafted cave, a mini-boss (like a Gleeok or Flux Construct), or a hidden shrine—training the player to trust their eyes instead of staring at a minimap.
    

### 2. What You Should Steal From _TotK_ for _Ashen Kata_

You don't need to build three stacked world maps like _TotK_ to capture why its exploration feels so good—just apply its three best reward patterns to your mountain-ringed regions:

1. **Hide "Beneath-the-Surface" Pockets Inside Ordinary Landmarks:**
    
    - Take _TotK_’s **wells and cave networks** idea: let an ordinary burned farmhouse cellar, a dry mountain well, or a crack behind a waterfall in your starting region open into a small underground shrine or smuggling tunnel containing a permanent combat art, weapon charm, or shortcut key.
        
2. **Reward Curiosity With Permanent Utility, Not Just Consumables:**
    
    - Like _TotK_’s Autobuild Schematics and Armor perks, put **permanent combat/traversal upgrades** (e.g., a deflection-window charm, a fire-ward talisman, or a prosthetic/secondary tool upgrade) in optional side areas—so players feel tangibly stronger for exploring off the main road.
        
3. **Use Visual Beacons to Guide Curiosity Naturally:**
    
    - Copy _TotK_’s **cherry blossom trees and smoke plumes**: place subtle, diegetic visual cues—like a lone glowing spirit-lantern, rising embers from a ruined watchtower, or a ribbon-wrapped stone cairn on a high ridge—wherever an optional secret or nocturnal elite encounter is tucked away.
